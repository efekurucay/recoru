Privacy Policy for Recoru

Last updated: March 2024

TL;DR: Recoru is an offline-first, local-only extension. It does not collect, transmit, or share ANY of your data, audio recordings, or browsing history with anyone. Minimum required permissions are used solely for the core functionality of the extension.

1. Data We Collect (None)

The Recoru Chrome Extension does not collect personal information, browsing history, analytics, telemetry, or any tracking data.

2. Microphone Usage

Recoru requires the microphone permission to record your voice/instrument when you play chords.

  • The microphone is only activated when you explicitly click the "Start Recording" button within the extension popup.
  • The microphone stops immediately when you click "Stop", or if you close the tab.
  • No background listening occurs whatsoever.

3. Where Your Data is Stored

All audio recordings and associated metadata (song names, recording labels, timestamps) are saved exclusively on your local device using your browser's built-in IndexedDB storage.

This data never leaves your computer. It is not synced to Google Drive, it is not sent to any developer servers, and it cannot be accessed over the internet.

4. Host Permissions

Recoru requests permissions for specific websites (e.g., `hakoru.net`, `repertuarim.com`). This permission is used strictly to read the current URL in order to identify the song you are viewing and organize your local recordings automatically. We do not inject ads, track your session, or read page contents other than the URL.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the extension's repository/page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.
